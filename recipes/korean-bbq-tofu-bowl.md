# Korean BBQ Tofu Bowl

## Ingredients

- 1 cup cooked quinoa or brown rice
- 4 oz tofu marinated in [B-Savory Sauce](/recipes/b-savory-sauce)
- 1/4 cup [Korean Tahini BBQ Sauce](/recipes/korean-tahini-bbq-sauce)
- 2 cups mixed baby greens
- 1/2 cup shredded carrots or beets
- 1/2 cup mung bean sprouts
- 1/2 cup sauerkraut or kimchi
- 1/2 avocado, sliced
- sesame or hemp seeds

## Directions

Warm the grains, tofu, and sauce and transfer to a bowl. Top with the remaining 
ingredients.
