# Blueberry Crumb Bars

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 teaspoons lemon zest
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup cold unsalted butter, cubed
- 1 large egg yolk

### Filling

- 3 cups fresh or frozen blueberries
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon lemon zest

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Butter a 9x13-inch pan.
2. In a food processor, pulse the flour, sugar, lemon zest, and salt together a
   few times to combine. Add the butter and pulse until you get a fine, sandy
   texture--all of the butter should be incorporated.
3. Add the egg yolk and pulse until it's combined, 10 to 15 times. The crumbs
   should hold together when squeezed in the palm of your hand. If they do not,
   sprinkle a tablespoon of water over top and pulse a few more times.
4. Scoop out about 1/3 of the crumb mixture and press it into a bowl, which
   helps to make larger clumps of crumbs for the topping. Cover and refrigerate
   at least 15 minutes or until needed for the topping.
5. Firmly press the remaining 2/3 of the crumbs into the bottom of the prepared
   pan to create the base for the bars. I like to use a measuring cup to press
   the crumbs into the pan and really compact the crust.
6. In a mixing bowl, toss the blueberries with the sugar, cornstarch, lemon
   juice, and zest together to coat. Spread the berries over the crust.
7. Remove the remaining crumb mixture from the fridge and crumble. Crumble it
   with your fingers and sprinkle over the blueberry filling.
8. Bake for 1 hour and 5 minutes (add 10 minutes if berries are frozen), or
   until the blueberries are bubbling and the crumb topping is golden. Remove
   the bars from the oven and transfer to a cooling rack. Cool completely in
   the pan, at least 2 hours, before cutting into squares. Once cooled and
   sliced the bars can be transferred to an airtight container and kept a room
   temperature for about a week.
