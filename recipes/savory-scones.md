# Savory Scones

## Ingredients

- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 3 tablespoon butter *cold*
- 1/3 cup milk *cold*

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 435°F and place a piece of parchment paper on a baking
   sheet.
2. Add the dry ingredients and butter to a food processor and pulse to combine.
3. Transfer to a bowl and add the milk. Mix using a spoon and your hands, but
   take care not to mix more than necessary. When you can create one cohesive
   ball from the dough, it's done.
4. Divide the ball into two equal sized balls, and place them on the baking
   sheet. Using your hands, flatten them to 4 inches across. Cut each on four
   pieces with a dinner knife and prick with a fork to create small holes on
   the surface.
5. Bake in the oven 8-10 minutes until slightly golden.
