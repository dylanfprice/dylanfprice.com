# Cream Cheese Frosting

This recipe makes enough to frost 24 cupcakes, or a 9x13-inch cake, or a
2-layer (9-inch) cake with a thin layer of frosting between the layers and on
top.

## Ingredients

- 2 (8 oz) packages cream cheese, softened
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 cups sifted confectioners' sugar

## Directions

1. Beat cream cheese and softened butter in a medium bowl until smooth and
   creamy. Mix in vanilla. 
2. Gradually add sugar, beating after each addition until incorporated.
   Continue to beat until frosting is very smooth.
