# Chocolate Cake

Bake as two 9-inch round layer cakes, a 9x13 cake, two dozen cupcakes, a Bundt
cake, or a sheet cake. Pair with [chocolate
frosting](/recipes/chocolate-frosting), [cream cheese frosting](/recipes/cream-cheese-frosting), or [peanut butter frosting](/recipes/peanut-butter-frosting).

## Ingredients

- 2 cups white sugar
- 1 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup milk
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 cup boiling water

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour two nine-inch round pans. Or
   line a half sheet pan with parchment paper and spray with cooking spray if
   you want to bake a sheet cake.
2. In a large bowl, stir together the sugar, flour, cocoa, baking powder,
   baking soda, and salt. Add the eggs, milk, oil, and vanilla. Mix for 2
   minutes on medium speed of mixer. Stir in the boiling water last. The batter
   will be thin. Pour evenly into the prepared pan(s).
3. Bake 50 to 55 minutes in the preheated oven until you can insert a toothpick
   into the cake, and remove it dry. Or, if you're baking a sheet cake, bake for
   only 15 minutes. Cool in the pan(s) for 10 minutes, then remove to a wire rack
   to cool completely.
