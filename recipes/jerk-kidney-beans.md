# Jerk Kidney Beans

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2 tablespoons [Jerk Spices](./jerk-spices)
- 1 14.5 oz can diced tomatoes with juice
- 2 15 oz cans red kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 12 oz marinara sauce or 14.5 oz can tomato sauce
- salt and black pepper
- cooked brown rice (1/2 cup per serving)

## Directions

1. Place a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the coconut oil. Once it
   melts, add the onion. Cook for 5 minutes, then stir in the jerk spices. Cook
   for 1 minute, then add the diced tomatoes with their juice, the beans, and
   marinara sauce. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook for 10 minutes.
2. Season with salt and pepper to taste, and serve over the rice.
