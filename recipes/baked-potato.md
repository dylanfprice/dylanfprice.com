# Baked Potato

## Ingredients

- 1 large (8-ounce) russet potato
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

## Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F.
2. Scrub the potato under warm water with a clean scrubbing sponge or brush. Pat dry. 
   Use your hands to rub the potato with oil. Sprinkle the skin evenly with 1/2 teaspoon 
   salt. Place the potato on a baking sheet (placing it directly on the oven rack could 
   lead to oil dripping and smoking). Bake until the potato is tender, 45 minutes to 1 
   hour; test it either by protecting your hand with an oven mitt or folded kitchen towel 
   and giving it a gentle squeeze, or check to see if a paring knife or fork slides in easily.
3. Let the potato cool for about 5 minutes, then cut a lengthwise slit in the middle. 
   Mash the exposed flesh with a fork. Sprinkle with a pinch of salt. Enjoy as is or 
   add your favorite toppings.
