# BBQ Spice Rub

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup paprika
- 1/2 tablespoon chili powder
- 1/2 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1/2 tablespoons onion powder
- 1/2 tablespoon salt
- 1/2 tablespoon ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)

## Directions

1. Mix all ingredients in an airtight container and store in the freezer.
